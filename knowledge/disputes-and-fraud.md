# Transaction Disputes and Fraud Reporting

What to do when a transaction is wrong, duplicated, or not made by you, and how Harbor Street Bank handles the dispute.

## Before raising a dispute

Check whether the merchant name on your statement is a trading name of a business you used, whether a family member
made the purchase, and whether the amount is a pre-authorisation that will be adjusted (common with hotels and fuel
stations). For a purchase you recognise but are unhappy with, contact the merchant first; most merchants resolve
refunds directly within 5 working days.

## Raising a dispute

A dispute can be raised in the mobile app from the transaction details, or through phone banking. You will need the
transaction date, amount and merchant name. Disputes must be raised within 60 days of the statement date on which the
transaction appears. Disputes raised after 60 days are reviewed case by case and may not be eligible for a refund under
card scheme rules.

## Provisional credit

For disputed card transactions above USD 25.00, a provisional credit for the disputed amount is applied to your account
within 10 working days of the dispute being accepted, while the investigation continues. If the investigation finds the
transaction was valid, the provisional credit is reversed and you are notified in writing at least 5 working days
before the reversal.

## Investigation timelines

Most disputes are resolved within 45 days. Disputes involving an international merchant can take up to 90 days under
card scheme rules. You can follow the status of a dispute in the mobile app under Help, My disputes.

## Reporting fraud

If you believe your card or account details have been stolen, block the card immediately (see the Card Blocking policy)
and report the fraud through phone banking, which is available 24 hours a day. The bank never asks for your full PIN,
one-time passcodes or online banking password by phone, email or text message; a request for these is itself a sign of
fraud. Fraud reports are acknowledged within 1 working day and investigated within 15 working days.
