# Transfer Limits, Charges and Cut-off Times

Limits and charges for money transfers from Harbor Street Bank personal accounts. Limits apply per account per calendar
day unless stated otherwise. Business accounts have separate schedules.

## Daily transfer limits

| Channel | Standard account | Premium account |
|---|---|---|
| Instant transfer (domestic, arrives within seconds) | USD 5,000 per day | USD 25,000 per day |
| Scheduled transfer (domestic, next working day) | USD 25,000 per day | USD 100,000 per day |
| International wire | USD 10,000 per day | USD 50,000 per day |
| ATM cash withdrawal | USD 1,000 per day | USD 3,000 per day |

Limits reset at midnight in the account's home time zone. Transfers between your own Harbor Street Bank accounts do not
count towards the daily limit.

## Raising a limit

Standard account holders can request a temporary increase of the instant transfer limit up to USD 10,000 for 24 hours
through the mobile app; the request requires a one-time passcode and is granted at the bank's discretion. Permanent
increases above the standard schedule are available only on Premium accounts. Limit increases cannot be processed by
chat or email.

## Charges

| Transfer type | Charge |
|---|---|
| Instant transfer | Free |
| Scheduled domestic transfer | Free |
| International wire, outgoing | USD 35.00 flat, plus any intermediary bank charges |
| International wire, incoming | USD 15.00 |
| Returned transfer due to incorrect beneficiary details | USD 20.00 |

Charges are debited at the time the transfer is processed and appear as a separate line on your statement.

## Cut-off times

Scheduled domestic transfers submitted before 16:00 on a working day are processed the same day. Transfers submitted
after 16:00, or on a weekend or public holiday, are processed on the next working day. International wires submitted
before 14:00 are released the same day; later submissions are released the next working day. Instant transfers have no
cut-off time.

## Failed and delayed transfers

If a transfer has not arrived within the expected time, wait until the end of the next working day before raising a
query, as receiving banks may apply their own processing times. After that, contact us with the transfer reference
shown in your transaction history. Funds from a failed transfer are returned to your account within 3 working days.
