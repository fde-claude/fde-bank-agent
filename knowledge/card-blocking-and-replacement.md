# Debit and Credit Card Blocking and Replacement

This policy describes how a Harbor Street Bank customer can block a lost, stolen or compromised card, what happens
next, and the fees and timelines that apply. It covers debit and credit cards issued by the bank.

## When to block a card

Block your card immediately if it is lost or stolen, if you see a transaction you do not recognise, or if a merchant
or ATM retained the card. Blocking is free and can be reversed within 24 hours if the card is found, provided it was
blocked as "temporary". A card blocked as "lost or stolen" is cancelled permanently and cannot be reactivated.

## How to block a card

You can block a card through any of these channels, 24 hours a day:

- The mobile app: Cards, select the card, Block card, choose Temporary or Lost/Stolen.
- Phone banking: call the number printed on the back of the card or shown on the Contact page of the website.
- Any branch during opening hours, with a valid photo ID.

Once blocked, all new transactions are declined within one minute. Recurring payments linked to the card number will
also be declined; the bank sends a list of affected merchants to your registered email so you can update them.

## Replacement card timelines

A replacement card is issued automatically when a card is blocked as lost or stolen. Standard delivery takes 5 to 7
working days to your registered address. Express delivery takes 2 working days and is available in metro areas.
A temporary block does not trigger a replacement.

## Fees

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Blocking a card (any channel) | Free |
| Replacement for a lost or stolen card, standard delivery | USD 10.00 |
| Replacement for a lost or stolen card, express delivery | USD 25.00 |
| Replacement for a damaged card | Free, once per 12 months |
| PIN re-issue | Free |

Fees are debited from the primary account linked to the card.

## Liability for unauthorised transactions

You are not liable for transactions made after you reported the card lost or stolen. For transactions made before the
report, your liability is limited to USD 50.00 if you report within 2 working days of noticing the loss, and USD 500.00
if you report within 60 days. See the Disputes and Fraud policy for the dispute process and timelines.
