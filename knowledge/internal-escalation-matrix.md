# Internal Escalation Matrix for Customer Service Staff

Internal document. Not for distribution to customers. Defines when and to whom customer-service staff escalate.

## Escalation tiers

Tier 1 is the first-line agent. Tier 2 is the team lead on duty, reached through the internal queue CS-TL. Tier 3 is
the specialist desk for the product concerned: Cards Operations (queue CS-CARDS), Payments (queue CS-PAY), or Disputes
and Fraud (queue CS-FRAUD). Executive escalations go to the Customer Relations Office (queue CS-CRO) and must be
logged with a case reference before transfer.

## Mandatory escalations

Escalate immediately, without attempting resolution at Tier 1: suspected fraud in progress, a customer reporting a
threat or coercion, any request involving a deceased account holder, a complaint mentioning a regulator or legal
action, and any transaction above USD 50,000 that the customer disputes.

## Service levels

Tier 2 call-back within 2 working hours. Tier 3 case acknowledgement within 1 working day and resolution within 10
working days. Executive escalations acknowledged the same working day. Breaches are reported weekly to Customer Service
Management.

## Goodwill payments

Tier 1 may offer goodwill credits up to USD 25.00 per case. Tier 2 may approve up to USD 150.00. Amounts above
USD 150.00 require Tier 3 or Customer Relations Office approval and a written justification in the case notes.
