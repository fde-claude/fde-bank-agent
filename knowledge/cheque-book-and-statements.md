# Cheque Books, Stop Payments and Account Statements

How to order cheque books, stop a cheque, and obtain account statements for Harbor Street Bank personal accounts.

## Ordering a cheque book

Cheque books are available in 25-leaf and 50-leaf formats. The first cheque book of each calendar year is free;
additional books are charged as listed below. You can order through the mobile app, phone banking or a branch.
Only one cheque book order can be open per account at a time; a new order can be placed once the previous one has been
delivered.

## Delivery

Cheque books are printed centrally and delivered by courier to your registered address within 7 working days. Delivery
to a branch for collection is available on request and takes 5 working days. Cheque books are not delivered to
addresses other than the registered address for security reasons.

## Charges

| Service | Charge |
|---|---|
| First cheque book in a calendar year (25 leaves) | Free |
| Additional 25-leaf cheque book | USD 5.00 |
| Additional 50-leaf cheque book | USD 8.00 |
| Stop payment on a single cheque | USD 15.00 |
| Stop payment on a range of cheques (lost cheque book) | USD 30.00 |
| Cheque returned unpaid due to insufficient funds | USD 25.00 |

## Stopping a cheque

You can stop a cheque that has not yet been presented through the mobile app or phone banking by quoting the cheque
number and amount. A stop payment takes effect immediately and remains valid for 6 months. If a cheque book is lost,
report it immediately so the full range can be stopped; the fee for a range covers all remaining leaves.

## Account statements

Monthly electronic statements are free and are available in the mobile app and online banking for the last 7 years.
Paper statements are posted on request and charged at USD 2.00 per statement. Interim statements for a custom date
range can be downloaded at any time at no charge. Statements for closed accounts can be requested at a branch and are
provided within 10 working days.
